# Free breakdown / training videos — the pre-sell layer

After the Q&A row, a few free-value videos breaking down **how they actually scaled specific
clients** — the full process running end to end on real people, so the viewer can see the
playbook working and picture themselves in it.

**Three by default**, one per featured client, under a header like
**"full breakdowns from real clients"**.

> **This is the block that makes someone arrive *ready to buy* rather than merely arrive.**
> The Q&A videos remove reasons not to buy. These build the reason to. A page with a strong
> Thank You video and no breakdowns gets a good show-up rate and a mediocre close rate.

---

## 1. Check what they already have before writing anything

**These do not need to be made from scratch, and re-using is the right answer.** Existing
material that works as-is:

- A YouTube client breakdown or case-study walkthrough they already published
- A long-form training already on their channel
- Their main VSL
- A free resource or workshop recording

Ask what they already have and which client each one covers. **Only script the gaps.** Writing
three fresh scripts for someone sitting on four published case studies wastes their afternoon.

If their VSL is not recorded yet, that is the **VSL** module's job, not this one. Do not
script a VSL here.

---

## 2. Do not over-produce them

Their only job is to show the system working on someone real. Production value works against
this — a polished case-study film reads as marketing, a founder walking through a real
account reads as true.

Same rule as everywhere else on this page: **clear beats slick.**

---

## 3. When one does need scripting

Five beats. Ten to twenty minutes is normal — these are allowed to be long, because the person
watching has already booked a call and is now deciding how much to want it.

1. **Name the client and the starting point.** Real name where they have permission, real
   numbers. *"[CLIENT NAME] came to us at [starting point]."* Pick the client whose starting
   point most resembles the viewer's.
2. **Name what was broken.** The specific constraint, not "they needed more leads". *"They had
   an offer that converted and no way to get in front of anyone."*
3. **Walk the process end to end.** The actual steps, in order, in the order they were run.
   This is the whole point of the block — the mechanism from the Thank You video, now shown
   operating on a real person rather than described.
4. **Show the artefacts.** Screen-share the ad account, the funnel, the CRM, the dashboard.
   Real screens, unpolished. The same instinct as the proof walkthrough.
5. **Land the result, then point at the call.** Before-to-after with the timeframe, then:
   *"if you want the version of this built for your business, that's what the call is."*

**Never write a client name, a starting number or a result the user did not give you.** Use
`[CLIENT NAME]`, `[STARTING POINT]`, `[RESULT]`, `[TIMEFRAME]` and tell them what is missing.

### Ask about permission

A named client breakdown with real revenue in it needs that client to be fine with it. Ask
before writing one; suggest first-name-only or "a client in [industry]" where they are not
sure. It is their relationship, not the skill's call to make.

---

## 4. Pick the three

If they have more than three clients to choose from, pick for **range**, not for the biggest
number:

- One that starts where most of their prospects start
- One that is the flagship result
- One that covers a different niche or a different objection, so the viewer cannot dismiss it
  as "that only worked because they were already big"

## 5. Shot list

- **Landscape.** These carry screen-shares and sit in a wide player.
- Screen-record over voice for most of it; face-cam optional and only if it is already how
  they film.
- One take, cuts allowed between sections if they are already comfortable editing. If not,
  one continuous run is fine — nobody is grading the transitions.
- **Have open before recording:** the client's ad account, funnel, dashboard or CRM — whatever
  actually shows the work.
- Unlisted YouTube or Loom. If it is an existing public YouTube video, the public link is fine
  — no need to re-upload.

## 6. Where they go on the page

One per featured client, inside **step 4** of the confirmation page —
`confirm.breakdowns[].video_url`, with `confirm.breakdowns[].title` for the client name and
`confirm.breakdowns[].result` for the headline number.

Adding more entries adds more cards.

## 7. Failure modes

- **Making them from scratch when four published videos already exist.** Ask first.
- **A highlight reel instead of a walkthrough.** The value is in seeing the process run, not
  the result announced.
- **Describing the process instead of showing the screens.**
- **Three breakdowns of the same client profile,** so anyone who doesn't match dismisses all
  three.
- **No call-to-action at the end.** Each one lands on the call, same as the Q&As.
