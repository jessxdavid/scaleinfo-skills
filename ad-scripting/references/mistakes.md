# Common mistakes

Run the whole batch against this list before handing anything over.

## 1. Closing the loop in the hook

*"Here's how to make $10K"* — the hook answers itself, so there is no reason to keep watching.
Give just enough to create curiosity, never the answer. Applies to Mode 1 and to every B2C
angle. In B2C specifically: **do not resolve the open loop** — if they get the answer in the ad,
they never click through to the VSL.

## 2. CTA too late or too early

- **Mode 1:** first CTA at the 15–20 second mark, final CTA at the end.
- **Mode 2:** a single CTA at the end only. No mid-roll CTA.

## 3. Generic numbers

*"Make good money"* instead of *"$10K/month"*. Specific beats vague every time — money figures,
timeframes, percentages, counts. If the user has not given you the number, use a visible
placeholder. Never estimate one.

## 4. AI slang

*"No stress. No fluff. Just results."* — an instant credibility killer. Any tricolon of clipped
negations belongs in the bin.

**The read-it-aloud test:** read every script out loud. If you would not say it in a
conversation, cut it. This catches AI slang faster than any checklist.

## 5. Wrong CTA for the mode

Do not use "comment KEYWORD" in a funnel ad. Do not use "click the link" in a DM ad.

This is the single most expensive mistake in the whole skill. A "comment KEYWORD" CTA on a VSL
funnel or a B2B call funnel **sends paid traffic nowhere** — the click never reaches the page,
the lead event never fires, and the spend is wasted. Being B2C is not a reason to use the comment
CTA. See `modes.md`.

## 6. Lifestyle in B2B ads

Cars and travel in a B2B funnel ad kill credibility with operators. Revenue, ROAS, CAC, clients
signed. Nothing else.

Lifestyle is permitted in Mode 1's Desire beat, and in B2C retargeting when tied back to the
mechanism. It is never permitted in a B2B ad.

## 7. No case studies

Both modes need social proof. Always include named results.

- **Mode 1 format:** *"[CLIENT NAME] went from [BEFORE] to [AFTER] in [TIME]"*, placed straight
  after the first CTA, for the sceptics still watching.
- **Mode 2:** at least three case studies, rapid-fire — name, before, after, timeframe. Proof,
  not storytelling.

## 8. Same hook across a batch

Every ad in a batch must have a unique opening angle. The hook is the variable being tested.

## 9. Invented client facts

Not in the original list, and the most damaging of all: a fabricated client name, revenue figure
or timeframe is a claim the user will read out on camera and run as a paid ad. Never generate
one. `[CASE STUDY 2 — NAME, BEFORE, AFTER, TIMEFRAME]` left visible in the script is always the
correct output when the number is not known.

The same applies to the guarantee. Do not sharpen a vague offer into a specific promise the user
never made.

## 10. Wrong ad copy format

The ad copy above the video is a separate deliverable from the script, and the two audiences want
opposite things.

- **B2B:** short, arrow bullets, guarantee headline, two case studies, "click below to see how".
- **B2C:** long-form, first person, story-led, reads like a personal note, warm sign-off. It
  never mentions a call, a price or an application.

Writing a punchy direct-response block for a B2C VSL funnel is the usual version of this error.

---

## Quick self-check

- [ ] Correct mode chosen — destination-led, not audience-led
- [ ] CTA matches the mode on every single ad
- [ ] A different hook on every ad, and different hook formulas across the batch
- [ ] No hook that closes its own loop
- [ ] Title hook on each, roughly 8 words, matching the opening spoken line
- [ ] All five formulas (B2B) or all four angles (B2C) represented
- [ ] At least one ultra-short ad in the batch
- [ ] Every number specific, and sourced from the user or clearly placeholdered
- [ ] No lifestyle content in any B2B ad
- [ ] Every script read out loud, AI slang cut
- [ ] Ad copy block written for every ad, in the right format for the audience
- [ ] Retargeting batch scripted too — 3–5 B2B, 5 B2C
- [ ] Every remaining placeholder listed for the user
