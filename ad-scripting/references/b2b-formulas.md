# B2B funnel ads — the five formulas

Mode 2 only. Every ad here ends in a link click, never a comment.

**Mix all five across a batch.** Never run five ads on the same formula. Include at least one
ultra-short ad (Formula 4) in every batch.

## Length guidelines

| Formula | Length | Words |
|---|---|---|
| 4 — Free Value Lead | 20–30s | ~50–80 |
| 3 — Results-Led | 45–60s | ~150–200 |
| 1 — Guarantee Lead | 60–90s | ~200–300 |
| 5 — Personal Story + System | 60–90s | ~200–300 |
| 2 — Problem Callout + System Reveal | 90–120s | ~300–400 |

Nothing in Mode 2 goes past 120 seconds or 400 words.

## Formula 1 — Guarantee Lead

Open with an irresistible guarantee, explain the system, stack case studies, CTA.

**Structure**

1. **Guarantee statement** — 5s
2. **ICP callout + qualification** — 5s
3. **System walkthrough** — 15–25s
4. **Case study stack** — 15–20s
5. **Guarantee recap + CTA** — 5–10s

**When to use:** done-for-you agencies, lead gen services, any offer with a quantifiable outcome.

**Worked example — Kraken Digital, 62s**

> TITLE HOOK: We guarantee $50K/month from Google Ads in 60 days
>
> We will add $50,000 a month to your business from Google Ads in 60 days, or we work for free
> until we do. And we'll do it all without you having to lift a finger. Let me explain. If you
> run a business doing over $1 million per year, you have everything you need right now to add
> $50,000 per month on Google Ads, and we'll build a system for you to do that in under seven
> days. We'll set up your entire Google Ads account from scratch, or overhaul what you already
> have. We'll then leverage your winning angles and concepts from all of your other marketing
> channels. And finally, we'll scale you until we add $50,000 per month in revenue from Google
> Ads, or we work for free until we do. This e-commerce brand took us up on our offer just a
> couple of months ago, and they've already added $133,000 in revenue to their brand in under
> 60 days. This Kitchenware brand added $61,000 per month in revenue in 28 days. It really is
> that simple. And the entire setup takes less than 15 minutes of work from your side, then we
> do the rest. Just click the link below, and I'll show you exactly how we'll add $50,000 per
> month in revenue to your business in 60 days, or we work for free until we do.

Note the guarantee appears three times: opening line, end of the system walkthrough, and the CTA.

## Formula 2 — Problem Callout + System Reveal

Lead with the ICP's core problem, position your system as the solution, prove it with results.

**Structure**

1. **ICP callout + problem** — 5–10s
2. **Anti-method stack** — 5–10s — what you do *not* do
3. **System reveal** — 15–25s — numbered steps
4. **Zero-risk framing** — 5s — "$0 down" / "pay after results"
5. **Case study stack** — 15–20s
6. **CTA** — 5s

**When to use:** offers where the *method* is the differentiator.

**Worked example — Leadscape Digital, 114s**

> TITLE HOOK: Everyone talks about building AI agents, nobody talks about getting clients
>
> AI automation agencies, everyone is talking about how to build AI voice agents, but nobody is
> talking about how to actually get clients. That's the hard part. Give me just 7 days and for
> $0 down, I'll install my AI acquisition system into your agency. Here is exactly how this
> works. For just $0 down, I'll implement our entire AI acquisition system completely done for
> you that will fill you or your closer's calendars with 6-8 qualified calls per day and scale
> your AI automation agency to $100k a month plus and you only need to pay me in full after you
> get the results with it. So if that interests you, you can click the link right below and book
> a call. And by the way, if you're interested in how this actually works, let me explain.
> Number 1, I'll build and install our entire AI acquisition system done for you into your
> business that will fill you or your closer's calendar with 6-8 qualified calls per day.
> Second, I'll work with you or your sales team to ensure that you're closing at least 40-50% of
> the clients from your high ticket offer. And finally, number 3, I'll let you test drive this
> entire process for $0 down and you only need to pay in full after you've gotten results with
> it. Connor came in at $20k a month. Last week alone, he did $120k a month and is now on track

> an AI automation agency and you want to close 1-2 clients per day, click learn more below,
> fill out the application and book a call.

Note the case study stack: four names, each one before → after → timeframe, no storytelling.

## Formula 3 — Results-Led (social proof forward)

Lead with client results immediately, then briefly explain how, CTA.

**Structure**

1. **Bold claim or guarantee** — 3–5s
2. **Aggregate proof** — 5s — "we've helped X clients generate $Xm"
3. **Case study stack** — 15–20s — named results with specific metrics
4. **Qualification** — 5–10s — two scenarios the viewer might be in
5. **CTA** — 5s

**When to use:** offers with strong social proof, many case studies, an established brand.

**Worked example — Daniel Iles / Viral Coach, 48s**

> TITLE HOOK: Here's what happened after 90 days
>
> Here's what happened to a few clients after 90 days of working with us. A defense attorney
> went from 344 followers to 230,000 followers, ranked in the top 4% of lawyers on social media
> worldwide right now. A home builder replaced 60,000 in ad spend with free organic traffic and
> now gets over 20 million views a month. A contractor closed multiple five-figure deals in his
> local area with just $242 in ad spend. That is a 61X return for him. A fitness coach got so
> many inbound leads that she had to hire an entire sales team just to keep up. But none of them
> did it themselves. All of them trusted our team with their social media, a team that had
> already done it hundreds of other times. And if you want results like that for your business,
> click the link down below.

## Formula 4 — Free Value Lead (ultra-short)

Give away something genuinely valuable for free. The ad is a gift that gets them into the funnel.

**Structure**

1. **Free offer statement** — 3–5s
2. **Credibility anchor** — 3–5s
3. **What's included** — 5–10s
4. **ICP callout** — 3–5s
5. **CTA + urgency** — 3–5s

**When to use:** personal brands, course and community funnels, top-of-funnel list building.
Ultra-short: 20–30 seconds only.

**Worked example — a free-value example, 25s**

> TITLE HOOK: Giving away my $30M system for free
>
> I am giving away the exact system that built my $30 million consulting business for free. Yep,
> free. The thing that took me 2,500 hours to build is now available for you for free. 80 hours
> of content. Literally the longest video is nearly five hours long. This is for serious agency
> owners, coaches, and consultants who actually want to build something real. If that is you and
> you want it for free, click below and grab access while it's still free.

## Formula 5 — Personal Story + System

Lead with your own experience, identify the problem you solved, reveal your solution.

**Structure**

1. **Personal credibility** — 5–10s
2. **The problem you found** — 10–15s
3. **What you built** — 10–15s
4. **How it works for them** — 10–15s
5. **Free resource + CTA** — 5–10s

**When to use:** newer agencies, authentic founder stories, done-with-you offers, lower-aggression
funnels.

**Worked example — The Cultivator, 66s**

> TITLE HOOK: The hardest part of scaling Shopify isn't ads
>
> I've scaled multiple of my own Shopify stores to five and six figure months, and there's one
> massive problem that stood out to me, and that wasn't ads or the product, it was finding my own
> team that could actually reliably execute the emails, ads and optimizations across the whole
> funnel. Every agency I talked to seemed to have one little bit of the funnel but didn't come
> across everything. So I simply built my own team and now we're scaling over 20 stores towards
> six figure months with the same funnel. I made a free video on this page breaking down exactly
> how we do it, and we're giving a select amount of businesses the opportunity to have a call
> with us. We don't do cookie cutter strategies, every single brand has its own method. So down
> below is a video breaking down how we do it. I would love to be on a call with you soon.

## Using the worked examples

Read them before writing, then leave them alone. They are structural models, not phrasebooks.

- **Never reuse their numbers.** `$50,000 a month`, `$133,000`, `Connor`, `$72k in 30 days` —
  all belong to those brands. Substitute the user's own case studies, or leave
  `[CASE STUDY 1 — NAME, BEFORE, AFTER, TIMEFRAME]` visible.
- Match the **beat timings**, the CTA position and the proof density, not the copy.
- The tone to copy is the one every example shares: a founder stating their numbers plainly.

## B2B retargeting

3–5 scripts per batch. Same formulas, aimed at people who already saw the offer and did not book.

**Audience:** website visitors from the last 30 days who did not book, plus video viewers (75%+)
who never visited the site. **Budget:** 15–20% of total ad spend.

Creative types, most effective first:

1. **Testimonial videos** — clients telling their own story, 30–60 seconds.
2. **Case study breakdowns** — one client's results with specific numbers.
3. **Objection-handling ads** — *"Worried it won't work for your niche? Here's what happened when…"*
4. **Behind the scenes** — dashboards, strategy calls, real work in progress.

Refresh **5–10 retargeting creatives monthly**, and collect testimonials continuously.
